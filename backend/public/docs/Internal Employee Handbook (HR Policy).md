# **EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK: HYBRID WORK & BENEFITS**

**Company:** GreenLeaf Logistics  
**Document ID:** GLL-HR-2024-B  
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2024

## **1\. HYBRID WORK SCHEDULE**

GreenLeaf Logistics operates on a tiered Hybrid Model based on department codes.

### **1.1 Department Tiers**

* **Tier A (Operations & Warehouse Management):** Must be on-site **4 days a week** (Monday through Thursday). Remote work is permitted on Fridays only.  
* **Tier B (IT, Engineering, Marketing):** Must be on-site **1 day a week** (Wednesdays are mandatory for "All-Hands").  
* **Tier C (Sales Representatives):** Fully remote is permitted, but Sales staff must visit the HQ once per quarter for planning.

  ### **1.2 Core Hours**

regardless of location, all employees must be reachable via Slack and Email during "Core Hours," which are defined as **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST**.

---

## **2\. HOME OFFICE STIPEND POLICY**

### **2.1 Eligibility & Amount**

Full-time employees are eligible for a "Home Office Setup Stipend" of up to **$1,200 USD**. This is a one-time reimbursement available after completing the **90-day probationary period**.

### **2.2 Refresh Cycle**

Employees may request a "Technology Refresh" stipend of **$500** every **3 years** (36 months) from their original hire date.

### **2.3 Approved vs. Prohibited Items**

* **Approved Items:** Monitors, ergonomic office chairs, standing desks, webcams, noise-canceling headphones, keyboards.  
* **Prohibited Items:** Gaming consoles, coffee machines, decorative lighting (RGB strips), gaming chairs (racing style), and home internet bills.

  ---

  ## **3\. TRAVEL AND EXPENSES (T\&E)**

  ### **3.1 Per Diem Rates**

* **Domestic Travel:** $60.00 per day.  
* **International Travel:** $90.00 per day.

  ### **3.2 Receipt Requirements**

* **Under $25:** Receipts are NOT required for individual meals or taxi rides under $25.00.  
* **Over $25:** You must upload a photo of the itemized receipt to the Expensify portal.  
* **Alcohol:** Alcohol is never reimbursable unless it is a client dinner with a Director-level approval.

  ### **3.3 Submission Deadline**

Expense reports must be submitted by the **25th of the current month** to be paid out in the next payroll cycle. Reports submitted on the 26th or later will be delayed by one month.

---

## **4\. PTO (PAID TIME OFF) ACCRUAL**

### **4.1 Accrual Rate**

* **Years 0–2:** Employees earn **1.25 days** of PTO per month.  
* **Years 3+:** Employees earn **1.75 days** of PTO per month.

  ### **4.2 Carry-Over Policy**

You may carry over unused PTO into the next calendar year, but there is a **"Hard Cap" of 10 days**. Any unused PTO above 10 days on December 31st will be forfeited.

---

## **5\. SECURITY PROTOCOLS**

### **5.1 Password Rotation**

Internal passwords for the logistics dashboard must be changed every **60 days**.

### **5.2 Public Wi-Fi**

Employees are strictly prohibited from accessing the "GreenLeaf Cargo Database" while connected to public Wi-Fi (e.g., airports, cafes) unless the corporate VPN (Cisco AnyConnect) is active.
